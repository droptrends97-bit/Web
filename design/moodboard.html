Why it works here

Flatters low-resolution photography — dark grounds forgive what monochrome exposes, which matters until there is a shoot. Gold earns its place from the menu rather than the mood board. Reads expensive on a phone at arm’s length.

The risk

Dark sites can feel cold for a service built on warmth, and Lise may simply not want black. Cinzel is capitals-only in practice — it cannot carry lowercase headings, so the type system has to be disciplined.

Why it works here

The most distinctive of the three by a distance, and the cheapest to build well. Monochrome makes the review count the loudest thing on the page, which is exactly where their advantage is. Ages slowly.

The risk

Unforgiving. Black-and-white photography exposes every flaw in a phone snap, so this direction is conditional on a proper shoot. Bodoni’s hairlines need care below 16px or they disappear on cheap screens.

Why it works here

Nearest to the cream-and-bronze identity they already own, so it is the easiest sell in the room and the least likely to be rejected on sight. All-serif is genuinely uncommon on salon sites and reads as heritage without costume.

The risk

Warm cream with a serif and an earth accent is the most-travelled road in this brief — it needs the asymmetric layout and the all-serif discipline to avoid looking like every other wellness page. Cormorant runs light; it needs weight 500 upward at body sizes.

Where I’d put the money: Direction 01.

Gold Leaf is the only one of the three whose palette is argued for rather than chosen. The gold isn’t decoration — it’s their own 24K Gold Leaf Facial, the most expensive thing on their menu and the one treatment with no shopfront anywhere online. Cinzel is within a hair of the letterspaced serif already above their door on Main Street. It flatters bad photography, which matters until there’s a shoot.

Direction 02 — if

Lise wants to look like the most serious salon in Wicklow and is willing to invest in photography first. Broadsheet is brutal about image quality: monochrome hides nothing.

Direction 03 — if

She reacts badly to dark backgrounds. Oxblood is the warmest, softest room of the three and the closest to the cream-and-bronze she’s already printing.

Still open

Whether Skin Deep is her mother’s salon — it changes the hero from a claim into a story. And how hard the audit section hits.
