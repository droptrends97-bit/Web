## Users

Homeowners (and some businesses) in Dublin, North Wicklow and surrounding counties who have a garden they value but no time, energy or expertise to keep it at its best. Typically comfortable, established households with mature, designed gardens: lawns, borders, topiary, containers. They arrive from referral, Google ("gardener Dublin", "garden maintenance"), Houzz or pickapro, usually on a phone, and want to know quickly: does he do what I need, is he good, is he trustworthy, how do I get a quote.

## Product Purpose

Darragh Connolly Garden Care is an owner-led garden care business. The site exists to turn a visitor into a phone call or quote request. Success is more enquiries of the right kind (ongoing maintenance and higher-value interventions), fewer enquiries for work he doesn't do (paving, paths, patios).

## Positioning

Garden care only, done by Darragh himself to a high standard, with a "wellness" framing: a garden as a place of peace and renewal, not just a landscape. He does not build or pave; he looks after gardens "like it's his own". Business grows through referral.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services (from the current site):
- **The Garden Intervention**: a one-off seasonal reset — plant cut-backs, weeding, mulching, power washing. Currently promoted as a special offer.
- **Garden Care & Maintenance**: recurring visits after an initial visit; schedule monthly, fortnightly or weekly. Visit options: Full day, 4 hours, 3 hours, 2 hours, 1.5 hours, all with 2 gardeners. No prices published.
- **What a maintenance visit includes**: pruning, weeding and general tidying; lawn edge care and light hedge trimming; border and bed maintenance; green waste removal (within reason). "We bring all the tools."
- **Garden Health**: soil and lawn conditioning, spraying and feeding programmes.
- **Pots and Planters** ("Passionate About Pots"): professionally designed seasonal container displays for entrances, patios, balconies and businesses; installation included, seasonal refreshes.
- **Bulb Planting**, **Wellness Planting**, **Hedging of all types** (named in footer/services list; detail pages not seen yet).
- **Does not do**: paving, paths or patios.

Service area: Dublin and North Wicklow mainly; "surrounding counties".

## Product Principles

1. One clear next step everywhere: call or request a quote.
2. Let his real gardens do the selling; photography leads, decoration follows.
3. Say what he does and doesn't do plainly, so the right clients self-select.
4. Proof early: accreditations and reviews near the top, not buried.
5. Fast and readable on a phone in daylight.
