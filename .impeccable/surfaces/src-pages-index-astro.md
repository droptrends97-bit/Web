# Home (src/pages/index.astro)

Scope: marketing site home; visitor mode Persuade. Audience: Dublin/North Wicklow homeowners with valued, mature gardens, mostly on phones. Action: call 087 225 9319 or request a quote. Proof: his own garden photography, ALCI + Guild of Master Craftsmen, testimonial, honest "we don't lay paving". Constraint: assets are screenshot crops until originals arrive.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A garden is looked after across a year, not in a visit. The site is an almanac of Darragh's year: the current month leads, the season colours the page. Refuses the category default of a dark-green template with a stock hero and a row of identical service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Printed almanac. Pale seed-paper ground, deep evergreen ink, logo lime as the living accent, one season colour that drenches a full-width field (autumn acer wine now; spring blossom, summer lawn, winter frost). Bricolage Grotesque display set big and condensed like woodtype almanac heads, Schibsted Grotesk text, tabular month numerals, hairline almanac rules, square photo plates with captions.

STORY: Visitor sees this month's work in their own kind of garden, understands he does care not construction, trusts the accreditations and real gardens, calls or asks for a quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead (logo, nav, tappable phone). Huge headline left over paper; wide photo plate of the bench garden right and bleeding; primary "Get a quote" and phone beneath the headline; full-width twelve-month ribbon at the viewport foot, current month active in the season colour.

FORM: Gardener's Year almanac, candidate 1 of 7 (user pick over assigned 3); seed e914893a (degraded roll). Signature interaction: the month ribbon; selecting a month swaps that month's work panel with a quick crossfade; season colour follows the real date.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
