Certified cancer care

Touch therapy for skin in treatment

Josephine is certified in cancer care. Using the Lindi Skin range, she offers a head-to-toe treatment designed around the side effects of chemotherapy, radiation and surgery — excessive dry skin, hand and foot syndrome, hair loss.

A gentle face wash and a soothing balm. Citrus and lavender serums. Hands and feet worked slowly, with attention to the nail beds. A warm towel compress, then a cool one.

Who you'll see

Josephine
O'Malley

Josephine has owned and run Soul of Beauty since 2015. She's ITEC qualified — one of the highest international qualifications in the industry — and trained as a beauty and holistic massage therapist.

She has worked in health farms, five-star spas and luxury salons, and for a number of leading skincare brands. She qualified as a reflexologist as student of the year in her class, and is registered with the NRRI. She is also certified in cancer care.

Originally from Mayo.
